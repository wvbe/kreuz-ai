## Gaps

- FR-001 touch gestures: descoped by the owner (mouse and trackpad only).
- FR-010 trade priorities: descoped by the owner (no trade-policy screen).
- SC-001 (partial): the map draws every kind as one instanced mesh and a 4096-entity layout is tested, but a frame rate needs a real GPU; not measurable in jsdom. Proposed approach: a Playwright run with a frame counter.
- SC-002 (partial): citizen, then its workstation through the why chain, then the recipe (now a link into the content browser) is three clicks, but no test walks it; the citizen panel does not name the workstation it works at. Proposed approach: show the workstation or posting of the active task as a link in the citizen overview.
- SC-006/FR-023 (partial): the load time is measured in jsdom without WebGL or the network; a cold start in a browser is not.
- The WebGL canvas itself is replaced by a stub in app tests; the three.js layers are tested with `@react-three/test-renderer`.
