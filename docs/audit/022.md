## Gaps

- FR-015 (partial): dangling references, duplicate ids, read-only registries and one-pass error reporting are loader errors; the spec 027 FR-010/FR-011 tier rules are corpus checks (`settlement/validateTierReachability.ts`), not loader errors, by D-15/D-16 (see D-232). Proposed approach if the owner wants them at load: call `validateTierReachability` from `loadContentPack`.
- SC-007 (partial): eating, sleeping, working and diplomacy happen unaided in the living-world, checkpoint-c and diplomacy runs; worship and socialising are covered by unit tests of the trees but no full run builds a chapel without a player. Proposed approach: a scenario that designates a chapel and checks faith need recovery.
