# scenarios

Scenario files (JSON) that drive the game headlessly: `{name, seed, options?, steps[]}`. The format is documented in [docs/CLI.md](../docs/CLI.md) and implemented in `src/game/api/scenario/`.

- `kernel-smoke.json` - new game, 200 ticks, pause/resume, speed, save/load round trip, command-log replay.
- `world-gen.json` - seed 42 small world: terrain classes (water, fertile, forest, stone, iron ore, road), job board and six settlers, save/load and replay.
- `living-world.json` - Checkpoint B: new game seed 42 Small, two game days (576 ticks) with a save/load in the middle: all six settlers alive, hunger above zero (they ate), full health, every settler has done tasks (wandered), command-log replay.
- `first-jobs.json` - task 3.1: seed 42 Small, one day of settlers claiming and finishing `fell.trees` jobs from the village board (history, logs and wages in inventories), save/load in the middle and command-log replay.
- `town-crier.json` - task 3.1c: seed 42 Small, the player posts a job on the user-managed village board (`PostJob`), the starting Town Crier (entity 7) carries it (`pending-updates`, `town-criers`), save/load while it walks, the update is applied on arrival and the crier is free again, command-log replay.
- `hauling.json` - task 3.2: seed 42 Small, two days: settlers fell trees and haul the logs into the starting stockpile chest (`stock`, `stockpiles` queries), save/load in the middle and command-log replay.
- `zones.json` - task 3.4: a stockpile zone designated around the starting chest by command (status active the same tick, `zone-at`), a zone material filter, save/load, deleting the zone and command-log replay.
- `bakery.json` - task 3.3: seed 42 Small with the `startingTier` village; `debugSpawn` builds a walled bakery room (six walls, a door, an oven), a grinding mill and a chest of wheat, a `bakery` zone is designated, two production orders (grind 4, bake 4) run for two days: both orders complete, bread is in storage (at least 4 of the 8, settlers eat some) (`stock`, `order`, `production-orders`, `workstations`), save/load and command-log replay.
- `build-bakery.json` - task 3.5: seed 42 Small with the `startingTier` village; a chest of stone, planks and nails (`debugSpawn`: the wood to planks to nails chain needs the sawmill and smithy of later content tasks), the player places six walls, a door and an oven by `PlaceWall` / `PlaceDoor` / `PlaceFurniture` and designates a `bakery` zone; the settlers supply and build (about 700 ticks), afterwards the oven exists, the queue is empty and the zone is active; save/load in the middle and command-log replay.
- `why-flow.json` - task 3.6: the bakery pieces of `bakery.json`; a bake order without flour is Blocked `MissingInput flour` (`explain`, `idle-blocked`, `noProducer`), once a grind order exists the producer is served, later `flow-of` shows bread produced, wheat consumed and the producer by `Recipe`; save/load in the middle and command-log replay.
- `farming.json` - task 3.7a: two chests are spawned (`debugSpawn`), a `farm_field` zone is designated over six cells of the village (three of them fertile soil), the settlers sow, the crops grow `cropGrowthTicks` (864) ticks, are harvested and hauled: after five days wheat is in the chest (`stock`, `crops`) and a farmer gained farming XP; save/load in the middle of growth and command-log replay.
- `mining.json` - task 3.7a: two chests are spawned, then two days of settlers mining iron ore and quarrying limestone on their own (the auto-posters post while the stock is below `oreLowStock` / `stoneLowStock`) and hauling it into the chests; save/load in the middle and command-log replay.
- `checkpoint-c.json` - Checkpoint C: new game seed 42 Small, then **player commands only** (no `debugSpawn`): a stockpile zone, two `farm_field` zones, two chests, a workbench, a grinding mill, six walls, a door, an oven and a `bakery` zone, a stone block order, then grind and bake orders; ten days (2880 ticks) with save/load in the middle and replay. Afterwards wheat, flour and bread were produced, bread was eaten (`flow-of`), bread is in a chest and all six settlers are alive. `tests/integration/checkpointC.test.ts` adds 14-day survival, "nothing Unexplained" and the starvation contrast; `tests/e2e/cli.test.ts` runs it through the real CLI process. `docs/PLAYING.md` is the same opening as a guide.
- `trade-ore-for-iron.json` - task 4.1, D-13: **player commands only**: the Checkpoint C opening (fields, chests, bakery), then on the first visit of the travelling trader the player orders ore sold (`TradeSell`), the settlers mine it as usual and carry it to the trader, the refined credit is exactly half the ore sold; an order for more ingots than the credit is refused, the exact amount is bought (`TradeBuy`) and hauled into the chests, the rest of the credit survives the trader's departure, save/load and the wait for the next visit, when it is spent too; command-log replay.
- `diplomacy.json` - task 4.2: **player commands only**: the settlement sends a gift to the wary Barony of Ashford, the barony's standing toward it rises, a second gift makes it friendly and the proposed trade agreement is accepted (`factions-diplomacy`, `directives`, `agreements`), a cancelled directive is refunded, save/load while an envoy is on the way, command-log replay.
- `tier-progress.json` - task 4.4: **player commands only**: the Checkpoint C opening (fields, bakery) and then a walled throne room with a table (about day 7 the zone is active and the milestone `throne-room-established` is recorded); `settlement-progress` shows population 6/8, dwellings 0/4 and the throne room 1/1, so the settlement stays a Hamlet here (`hamlet-to-village.json` adds the dwellings and the settlers); `unlocks` and `milestones` queries, save/load and command-log replay. `tests/integration/settlement.test.ts` adds the promotion with the dwelling hook and two more settlers.
- `hamlet-to-village.json` - task 4.5, Checkpoint D: **player commands only**: the Checkpoint C opening plus a sawmill, then food orders, planks, 40 nails from the trader, a throne room and four 2x2 dwellings (a door and two beds each; the cells were found with a layout search) with the supply chain's priorities (food 60, rooms 46-43, planks 38, stone 36). The founders move into the rooms as they are finished, two settlers arrive on day 31 (at most two a day) and on day 32 (tick 9216) the settlement becomes a Village (`settlement-progress`, `housing`, `dwellings`); save/load in the middle and state-hash checks. `tests/integration/housing.test.ts` adds the events (one `settlement.tier.reached`, arrivals on the evaluation tick, nobody died, nothing Unexplained) and the second run.
- `standing-orders.json` - task 4.3, D-59: **player commands only**: the Checkpoint C opening with one chest and the throne room placed at once, a standing order for 20 bread (priority 80) and one for 12 flour (priority 70), the Steward appointed and one manual order for 8 stone blocks; difficulty `peaceful`. The throne room is active on day 2, the Steward reviews daily from tick 648 and every later bread and flour order is one of his runs (a Town Crier delivers it); bread never falls below 8 over ten days, all six settlers live; save/load, state-hash checks and command-log replay. `tests/integration/standing.test.ts` adds the review times, the audience tasks, saves at five phases and the "no manual food order" check.
- `chronicle.json` - task 4.6, D-60: **player commands only**: the `tier-progress.json` opening (fields, bakery, throne room) with the Steward appointed (`AppointSteward`) and one citizen renamed (`RenameCitizen`); two weeks, then the chronicle holds the two `took_office` moments (Reeve, Steward), the throne-room milestone and the finest-citizen moments, the journal of the renamed citizen reads `arrived`, `renamed`, and the offices show in the styled names (`chronicle`, `journal`, `identity-of`); save/load in the middle, state-hash checks and command-log replay. `tests/integration/chronicle.test.ts` adds once-only recording, the levels, SC-005 along `hamlet-to-village.json` (the village promotion is a `tier_reached` entry) and saves at two phases.
- `gathering-sources.json` - task 5.3 part 2a: **player commands only**: a stockpile zone, two chests, a `flax_field` over six village cells and a `fishing_dock` on the shore; the settlers sow flax and fish, raw fish is produced from the first day (`flow-of`) and flax grows 432 ticks and is hauled into the chests (`stock` on day 6); save/load, state-hash checks and command-log replay.
- `fauna.json` - task 5.3 part 2b, D-140: **player commands only**: a new seed-42 Small game has wild animals (`animals`: the deer of entity 19, rabbits, foxes; no livestock) and they move over a day; the player posts a `hunt.game` job for the deer (`PostJob` with `entityId`), a settler chases and catches it and carries six `raw_meat` and two `raw_hide`; the animals are not part of the population; save/load, state hashes and command-log replay.
- `determinism.json` - mixed commands and steps with state-hash checks, replay before and after a save/load.

Run one with `npm run cli -- --script scenarios/kernel-smoke.json`. `tests/e2e/scenarios.test.ts` runs every `*.json` file in this folder in-process, so a new scenario is picked up automatically: later phases add their acceptance scenarios here (for example the Checkpoint C bakery scenario). Deliberately failing fixtures belong in `tests/e2e/fixtures/`, not here.
