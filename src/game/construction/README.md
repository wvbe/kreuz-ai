# src/game/construction

Construction (spec 016, DECISIONS D-27 and D-50, plan task 3.5). The player places blueprints (`PlaceFurniture`, `PlaceWall`, `PlaceDoor`, ...); each blueprint is a `build_site` entity on its cell that holds the staged materials; settlers carry materials into it (`build.supply` jobs, reservations on the stock) and one builder works on it (`build.construct`); on completion the site is replaced by the real entity. Deconstruction and cancel run through the same site. The engine registers it for itself (`registerConstruction` in the `GameEngine` constructor, after production).

- `constructionTypes.ts` - ids and constants (`constructJobId`, `supplyJobId`, `constructionPosterIntervalTicks` 6, `recentRetentionTicks` 288 ...), the enums `SiteKind` (`Construction`, `Deconstruction`), `SiteStatus` (`Planned, Supplying, Building, Done, Cancelled`), `PlacementReasonKind`, `ConstructionBlockedKind`, `BuildSiteData`, `RecentJob`, `PlacementResult` and the `construction.job.*` event names and payloads.
- `buildSiteComponent.ts` - the `BuildSite` component (strict Zod schema; the `build_site` prototype carries it next to a `queryable: false` staging `Inventory`). `ConstructionError.ts` - `ConstructionError` / `ConstructionErrorKind` (the command errors of section 3.3).
- `constructionDefinitions.ts` - the build definition is a **furniture record** of the content pack (`constructionMaterials`, `constructionTicks`, `unlockTier`, `deconstructionYield`, `removable`; `wall` and `door` are records of the same table): `findBuildDefinition`, `requiredMaterials`, `deconstructionTicks`, `unlockTierOf`, `isUnlocked`, `unlockText`, `builtPrototype`.
- `placement.ts` - `validatePlacement(engine, prototypeId, mapId, cellIndex)` (structured reasons, the query `validate-placement`) and `assertPlacementValid` (reason to command error).
- `buildSiteQueries.ts` - `listSites`, `findSite`, `requireSite`, `siteAt`, `siteTargeting`, `deliveredOf`, `missingMaterials`, `siteCell`, `siteWorkCell`.
- `constructionSites.ts` - the command logic: `queueConstruction`, `queueWalls`, `queueDeconstruction`, `cancelSite`, `setSitePaused`, `setSitePriority`, `moveSiteToFront`, `withdrawSiteWork`, `cancelJobTask`, `buildingIdOf`.
- `supplyExecutor.ts` / `constructExecutor.ts` - the executors of `build.supply` and `build.construct`. `constructionPoster.ts` - the slot-8 poster `postSiteJobs` and the sweep `sweepSites` (+ `refreshSiteStatus`).
- `siteCompletion.ts` - `completeSite` (place or remove), `placementBlockers`, `isSiteLost`. `siteRefund.ts` - `refundSite`, `destroySite` (the delete hook), `spawnLoosePile`, `dropLoosePile`. `wallObstruction.ts` - `applyWallObstruction`, `clearWallObstruction`, `rebuildWallObstructions`.
- `siteBlockers.ts` - `siteBlockers` (blocked reasons for spec 025). `constructionViews.ts` - `buildSiteDetail`, `buildQueueView`, `buildMenuView`.
- `ConstructionService.ts` / `constructionServiceRegistry.ts` - the per-engine list of recently finished jobs (section `systems.construction`); `getConstructionService(engine)`.
- `registerConstruction.ts` - `registerConstruction(engine)`: component, section, executors, delete hook, wall obstruction wiring, slot-8 system `construction`, commands and queries. `testConstructionWorld.ts` - test helper: a production test world with `place`, `site`, `hasSite`, `stockFor`, `runUntil`, `built`.

## Rules

- **Job = site.** The job id of every construction command and event is the entity id of the `build_site`. A site is `Planned` (nothing delivered, nobody on the way), `Supplying` (something delivered, a supplier or a posting out), `Building` (every material delivered, or a deconstruction); `Done` and `Cancelled` only live in the queue history (the entity is gone). A blueprint is no furniture and no wall: zones ignore it and walls obstruct only when finished.
- **Placement (016 FR-016/017, 024 FR-014).** Hard constraints: the map and cell exist, the definition exists, its tier is reached (`TierLocked`, text `Unlocks at <Tier>`, tier source = `JobService.currentTier()`, until 4.4 the `startingTier` option), the terrain is buildable (content `buildable`), no furniture, wall, door, board or other blueprint holds the cell. Citizens and creatures never block; zones are no constraint (`zoneId` is reported). `validatePlacement` returns every reason; the commands throw the DECISIONS error of the first one (`ContentLocked`, `LocationBlocked`, `LocationAlreadyOccupied`, `OutOfBounds`, `UnknownPrototype`). `QueueWalls` is all or nothing.
- **Routing (D-08).** Site postings go at once to the nearest running board of the site's map, with the site's priority and urgency and the usual adult-citizen eligibility. Every 6 ticks, per site that is not paused and has no posting out: a construction that lacks materials gets **one** `build.supply` posting for the first missing material that has a source (so one supplier per site, deliveries never overshoot); a site with every material and no builder gets **one** `build.construct` posting (one builder per site, FR-012). No source for any missing material: `construction.job.suspended` (`MissingInput`), resumed by `construction.job.resumed` when stock appears.
- **Supply.** The supplier reserves what it can carry of the nearest source (`ReservationKind.Supply`), walks there, commits the reservation into its own inventory, walks to the site and deposits at most what the site still needs into the staging inventory (queries ignore it). Anything left over, or carried when the trip is cancelled, is dropped as a `loose_pile` where the supplier stands; the haul poster takes it to storage. Every step keeps goods in the source, under a reservation, in a pocket or on the site: nothing is lost or duplicated (the tests count all materials over 1000 ticks with cancels).
- **Build.** The builder walks to the work cell (the site cell; for a wall that is taken down the first traversable neighbour), fixes the duration `workDuration(content, builder, build.construct, constructionTicks)` (skill `construction`; deconstruction takes `max(1, floor(ticks / 2))` base ticks), emits `construction.job.started` and waits that many ticks. The wrapper then emits `skill.work.completed` for `construction` once. A builder that is deleted, loses the claim or is cancelled puts the site back to "nobody works" with `progress` 0 (FR-011); the delivered materials stay.
- **Completion.** Construction: the staged materials are consumed (surplus is refunded), the entity is spawned on the cell (the engine prototype with the definition id, else `furniture_piece` with `Furniture {furnitureId}`; workstations carry `ProductionOrders`), a wall obstructs the cell (`BlockReason.Wall`, `map.setObstruction`, so paths re-plan) and the site is deleted. Zones (slot 9) see the new piece in the same tick. A door is passable but encloses a room. Deconstruction: the target is deleted (a workstation drops its contents, a wall frees its cell at once), the `deconstructionYield` lands as a loose pile. A site whose cell was taken (or whose target vanished) is cancelled by the sweep (`location_lost`).
- **Cancel (FR-014, D-27).** `CancelConstructionJob`/`CancelConstruction` works at any stage: a supplier or builder task is cancelled, the posting withdrawn, the staged materials go to the best storage (`chooseRoute`, else a loose pile on the cell), the site is deleted and the cell is free at once. A site deleted by any other means refunds through the before-delete hook.
- **Walls.** Every `wall` entity obstructs its cell: the `entity.spawned` event, the completion step and an init step after `newGame`/`loadGame` (`rebuildWallObstructions`) derive the obstruction from the entities; the before-delete hook clears it. Zone evaluation ignores the obstruction of a wall that is flagged for deletion this tick.
- **Not done.** Tools: the `iron_hammer` of the job type is not required of builders (016 US3 needs the tool-storage mechanic of a later task). The `NoQualifiedWorker` reason and multi-cell prototypes are not modelled.
