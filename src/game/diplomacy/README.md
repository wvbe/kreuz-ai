# src/game/diplomacy

Diplomacy between the settlement and the other factions (spec 021, DECISIONS D-14 and D-56, plan task 4.2): NPC factions that live off the map, standing deltas and decay, acts carried by envoys, gifts with a refund, trade agreements, proposals, incidents, leader succession. The engine registers it for itself (`registerDiplomacy` in the `GameEngine` constructor, after trade). Membership, the `Faction` component, `getStanding` / `setStanding` and the attitude bands live in [factions](../factions/README.md); this folder builds the rules on top.

- `diplomacyTypes.ts` - ids, the stream names `diplomacy.ai` and `diplomacy.resolve`, the enums `DiplomaticActType`, `DeclarationKind`, `EnvoyStatus`, `DispatchFailureReason`, `ProposalResponse`, `IncidentKind`, the types `EnvoyData`, `CargoItem`, `Proposal`, `NpcActRecord` and every event name and payload (`diplomacy.*`).
- `envoyComponent.ts` - the `Envoy` component (sender, target, act, declaration, cargo, trip ticks, status, failure) with a strict Zod schema; it lives in the entities save section.
- `DiplomacyService.ts` / `diplomacyServiceRegistry.ts` - the open proposals, the NPC act cooldowns and the hostility multiplier of the difficulty, saved in the section `systems.diplomacy`.
- `factionSeats.ts` - `pickSeatCell` (the extreme cell of a map edge), `seatOf` (the stored seat, or the market cell for the government) and `travelTicks` (distance over `envoyUnitsPerTick`).
- `npcFactions.ts` - `spawnNpcFactions` (new game: every content faction with an `npc` block gets its entity, a seat, a leader, an heir and the starting standing) and `spawnNpcMember`.
- `standingRules.ts` - the arithmetic: `giftDelta`, `applyGift`, `applyAcceptance`, `applyDeclaration`, `adjustStanding`, `scaleHostileDelta` (difficulty), `decayStandings`, `isNpcFaction`.
- `agreements.ts` - `setAgreement` (flag on both lists, `diplomacy.agreement.formed` / `.cancelled`), `hasAgreement`, `listAgreements`.
- `envoys.ts` - `validateDispatch`, `dispatchAct` (moves a gift into the envoy's cargo, spawns the envoy), `refundCargo`, `listEnvoys`, `pendingEnvoysOf`, `giftValueCoins`.
- `envoyTrips.ts` - `runEnvoys` (delivery to the current leader, waiting, timeout, return), `failEnvoy`, `cancelEnvoy`, `onEnvoyDeleted`.
- `actResolution.ts` - `resolveAct` (what a delivered act does), `acceptsAgreement`, `acceptsOverture`, `applyAccepted`.
- `proposals.ts` - `receiveProposal`, `respondToProposal`, `expireProposals`.
- `npcAi.ts` - `runNpcFactions` (the AI, one evaluation per faction every 72 ticks), `npcChoices`, `npcRecordOf`. `applyIncident.ts` - the insult. `runSuccession.ts` - the leader succession pass.
- `runDiplomacy.ts` - `runDiplomacy` (the slot-11 pass) and `isDecayTick`.
- `diplomacyViews.ts` - the views behind the queries `factions-diplomacy`, `directives`, `agreements`, `envoys`, `proposals`. `DiplomacyError.ts` - command failures.
- `registerDiplomacy.ts` - the component, section, init, before-delete hook, slot-11 system `diplomacy`, commands `IssueDiplomaticAct`, `CancelDiplomaticDirective`, `RespondToProposal` and the queries. `testDiplomacyWorld.ts` - test helper: a trade world with the NPC factions and a government leader.

## Rules

- **NPC factions** are content factions with an `npc` block (`side`, both starting standings, act weights, `warLike`): `merchant_caravans` (neutral), `ashford_barony` (wary, aggressive, war-like) and `wulfric_abbey` (friendly, pious). They are seeded at `newGame` after the world generator: the faction entity, a **seat** on the map edge of its side (no walking: seats are abstract), a leader and an heir (citizens without a position, named by `assignIdentity`, so they are not settlers, never counted or given jobs). A game without a map has none.
- **Standing** is `-100..100`, asymmetric. Bands (`Attitude`): Hostile below -30, Wary below 0, Neutral below 20, Friendly below 70, Allied. The derived hostile status (`isHostilePair`) is the trade gate (`faction-hostile`) and, through the mean standing of a worker's factions, the labour gate. `diplomacy.standing.changed` on every change, `diplomacy.attitude.changed` when the band changes. Decay: every second day (`standingDecayIntervalDays`) each non-zero value moves one point toward 0.
- **Acts** (`IssueDiplomaticAct`): a gift (coins and goods of the treasury, +`min(25, 5 + floor(value / 20))` to the receiver's view, half to the giver's), a trade agreement (accepted iff the target's standing toward the sender is at least 20 and neither side is hostile: +10 both, flag on both, 0.9 price multiplier at that faction's traders), a declaration (war: both views at most -60 and the agreement ends; peace: at least -10; neutrality: 0) and an overture (accepted at -15 or better: +5 both; otherwise the target's view of the sender falls by 3). Refused when the target is leaderless, a trade agreement is proposed to a hostile faction or when one stands, the treasury lacks the gift, or the sender already has `maxEnvoysPerFaction` (4) envoys under way.
- **Envoys** are entities (`Envoy`, cargo inventory): the trip takes the seats' distance over `envoyUnitsPerTick` plus a jitter drawn from `diplomacy.resolve`; at `arriveTick` the message goes to the target's **current** leader (a leader appointed on the way receives it; a faction without leader makes the envoy wait). The only failure is the timeout `envoyStuckTimeoutTicks` (576) after the dispatch: `unreachable` while on the way, `leader-unavailable` while waiting at a leaderless faction or when the target faction was destroyed; `envoy-destroyed` only for an entity deleted from outside. A failed or cancelled gift goes back to the treasury at once (or as a loose pile at the market when the treasury cannot take it). The sender's leader dying does not stop the trip.
- **NPC AI** (`diplomacy.ai`): an NPC faction with a leader evaluates when `tick mod 72 == factionId mod 72` and at most once per 288 ticks. A war-like faction at -20 or worse (above war) declares war with chance `warChance` (10 %) x hostility multiplier; otherwise it draws by weight an overture, an agreement proposal (envoy to the player's leader, answered with `RespondToProposal`; unanswered it lapses after 576 ticks) or an incident (an insult: the settlement's view -5, its own view of the settlement -2, both scaled by the difficulty, gated by `incidentChance`). The hostility multiplier (peaceful 0.25, steady 1, harsh 1.5) scales every negative delta an NPC faction applies toward the player and the incident weight.
- **Succession** (slot 11, every tick): a faction whose `leaderId` is null picks `pickLeaderCandidate` (greatest total skill, ties lowest id); a faction without members stays leaderless, except an NPC faction, which gets a new heir. `SetFactionLeader` (in the factions system) sets or clears a leader. The styled name of a leader shows the office (identity reads `Faction.leaderId`), so no separate title update exists.
- All state is integers; every draw is on a named stream; envoys, proposals, cooldowns and standing round-trip through save/load (a game saved while an envoy is under way continues identically).
