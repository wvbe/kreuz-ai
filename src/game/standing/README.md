# src/game/standing

Standing orders and the Steward (spec 026, DECISIONS D-19 and D-59, plan task 4.3). The player says "keep N of this material in stock"; once a day (slot 14, `stewardReviewTickOfDay` = 72, right after the housing evaluation of slot 13) the appointed Steward counts the stock and, with hysteresis, queues single-craft runs that a Town Crier delivers; the runs become ordinary production orders. A Notice Post and a Bell Tower shorten the delivery. The engine registers it for itself after housing (`registerStanding` in the `GameEngine` constructor).

- `standingTypes.ts` - the system id, the task `govern.steward_audience` and its priority 70, the enums `StandingOrderScope`, `StandingOrderState`, `RunStatus`, `StandingOrderErrorKind`, `StewardVacancyReason`, `ReviewSkipReason`, the data types `StandingOrder`, `OwnedRun`, `StewardshipState` and the 12 event names with payload types. `StandingOrderError.ts` - the typed command failure (the message starts with the kind).
- `StandingService.ts` / `standingServiceRegistry.ts` - the saved state (root key `stewardship`: orders with the hysteresis bit `restocking`, owned runs, `nextOrderId`, `nextRunId`, the Steward, his board, the extra-review request, the last review tick). Nothing derived is saved.
- `resolveRecipe.ts` - material and/or recipe to `{materialId, recipeId, outputPerRun}` (`NoProducingRecipe`, `AmbiguousRecipe` with candidates, `ContentLocked`). `standingOrders.ts` - `createStandingOrder`, `updateStandingOrder`, `setStandingOrderPaused`, `deleteStandingOrder`, `defaultThreshold`, `dropFinishedOrders` (the cap, the duplicate rule, the validation).
- `countStock.ts` - the stock an order counts (FR-006): storage furniture without household storage and loose piles, workstation inventories unreserved and haul couriers' loads (settlement scope only), or the furniture on the tiles of a zone. `hysteresis.ts` - the pure `stepHysteresis` and `desiredRuns`.
- `ownedRuns.ts` - a run is one craft: `queueRun` (a Steward-origin pending update on a user-managed board), `applyRun` (what the crier's delivery does: `createProductionOrder` of quantity 1 at the priority of the order), `runStatus` (`PendingAdd` / `Open` / `Claimed`, derived), `withdrawable` / `withdrawRun` (pending adds first, then open runs, newest first, never claimed ones), `pruneRuns`.
- `runStewardReview.ts` - the review: with a Steward and an active throne room the Steward gets the audience task and the orders are evaluated by priority (highest first, then lowest id): hysteresis, wanted runs, withdrawals, new runs on the resolved board; paused orders, missing zones and locked recipes only withdraw or do nothing. No Steward or no seat: `steward.review.skipped`, the runs are left alone. `findSeat.ts` - the lowest active throne room. `resolveBoard.ts` - the board of an order: its own, the Steward's, else the user-managed board with the cheapest walk from the throne room (path cost, ties lowest id).
- `steward.ts` - `appointSteward` (adult citizen of the player faction, no Town Crier; a sitting Steward is `Replaced`), `dismissSteward`, `setStewardBoard`, `requestStewardReview`, `checkStewardOffice` (`Died`, `LeftFaction`). `createAudienceTask.ts` - the task `govern.steward_audience`: walk to the throne room, stay `stewardAudienceTicks`.
- `preferredZone.ts` - the zone a restocking zone-scoped order steers its material into (installed with `StorageService.setZonePreference`, spec 026 FR-020; routing ranks it first and keeps the other routes as fallback).
- `deliveryRouting.ts` - `servingPost` / `noticePostRoute` (hop distance within `noticePostRadius`, nearest post, ties lowest id; installed with `CrierService.setRouter`), `activeBellTowers`, `ringBells` (at `bellRingTicksOfDay`, every update within `bellRadius` hops of the church bell is applied with `via: BellTower`). `hopDistance.ts` - the breadth-first hop count.
- `standingReasons.ts` - why an order does not progress (`Paused`, `NoSeatOfGovernment`, `NoSteward`, `LockedByTier`, `ScopeZoneMissing`, `NoReachableJobBoard`, `AwaitingTownCrier`, and production's `MissingInput` / `MissingWorkstation` / `NoQualifiedWorker` ... for a restocking order; a missing input another standing order makes points at that order) and the derived `standingState`. `standingProvider.ts` - the status provider of `StatusSubjectKind.StandingOrder`. `standingViews.ts` - the queries `standing-orders`, `standing-order {orderId}` and `steward`.
- `registerStanding.ts` - the slot-14 system (prune, office check, review, bells), the commands `CreateStandingOrder`, `UpdateStandingOrder`, `PauseStandingOrder`, `ResumeStandingOrder`, `DeleteStandingOrder`, `AppointSteward`, `DismissSteward`, `SetStewardBoard`, `RequestStewardReview`, the crier hooks and the audience task. `testStandingWorld.ts` - test helper: a housing world with a user-managed board, `crier`, `steward`, `standing`, `claim` / `finish` of runs, `runToReview`.

## Rules

- **State (D-19)**: an order starts `Satisfied` (`restocking = false`); the first review flips it when `stock <= threshold` (inclusive) and it stays `Restocking` until `stock >= target`. The shown state is derived: `Paused` beats `Blocked` (a primary reason other than `Paused`) beats `Restocking` / `Satisfied`; the hysteresis bit lives underneath.
- **Runs**: wanted runs = `min(maxOpenRunsPerOrder, ceil((target - stock) / outputPerRun))` while restocking, 0 otherwise. Goods still being crafted are not in the stock, so owned runs (claimed ones too) are compared to the wanted number; claimed runs are never withdrawn. A run is a production order of one craft: the poster of production puts its `craft.produce` posting on the nearest board as for any order.
- **Delivery**: a run is a pending board update of origin `Steward` (a change of kind `Run`) and waits for a Town Crier like a player's posting; a Notice Post that serves the board takes the crier there instead (`via: NoticePost`), a ringing Bell Tower applies the updates of boards within its radius (`via: BellTower`). The Steward is no Town Crier and no Town Crier can be Steward.
- **Distance (D-19)**: the board of an order is chosen by path cost from the throne room; the reach of a Notice Post and of the bell counts hops on the cell adjacency graph.
- **Defaults (conflict 11)**: `maxOpenRunsPerOrder` 5, `maxStandingOrders` 50, `noticePostRadius` 12 hops, `bellRadius` 25 hops, `defaultRestockFraction` 0.75, `stewardAudienceTicks` 12, `bellRingTicksOfDay` 72, 108, 144, 180, 216.
- **Determinism and saves**: no randomness anywhere; all numbers are integers; a game saved at any point resumes identically (orders, runs, pending updates and the Steward are saved).
