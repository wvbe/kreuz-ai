# src/game/housing

Dwellings and household upgrades (spec 029, DECISIONS D-28 and D-58, plan task 4.5). A dwelling is a zone of type `dwelling` (a walled room with a bed) that carries a `Dwelling` state; once a day (slot 13, `housingEvaluationTickOfDay` = 72) the evaluation houses the homeless, feeds the households, checks what each dwelling needs to rise or keeps it from falling, collects rent and lets settlers in. The engine registers it for itself after the status system (`registerHousing` in the `GameEngine` constructor).

- `housingTypes.ts` - the system id, the stream `housing.immigration`, the event names (`housing.dwelling.upgraded` / `.downgraded` / `.at-risk`, `housing.resident.assigned` / `.evicted`, `housing.rent.collected` / `.unpaid`, `housing.goods.consumed`, `housing.immigrant.arrived`, `housing.immigration.blocked`), the enums `DwellingRequirementKind`, `EvictionReason`, `RentShortfallReason`, `ImmigrationBlockedReason`, `SupplyStatus`, and the data and view types.
- `dwellingComponent.ts` - the `Dwelling` component `{level, upgradeStreak, downgradeStreak, consumptionAccumulators, foodRecord, lastEvaluatedDay}` (saved with the entities; requirement satisfaction is never saved). `ensureDwellings.ts` - `ensureDwelling` / `ensureDwellings` (a Hovel is created when the zone becomes active).
- `dwellingLevels.ts` - the ladder (`orderedLevels`, `levelRank`, `nextLevelOf`, `previousLevelOf`, `isAtOrAbove`), `levelDefinition`, `levelName`.
- `dwellingZones.ts` - `dwellingOf`, `listDwellings` (ascending zone id), `dwellingFurniture`, `dwellingStorage`, `dwellingBeds`, `dwellingCapacity` (`min(level capacity, beds)`). `household.ts` - `isEligibleResident`, `residentsByDwelling`, `residentsOf`, `homelessCitizens`, `assignHome`, `clearHome`.
- `dwellingRequirements.ts` - `levelRequirements` (MinTiles, Furniture, FoodVariety, ServiceNearby, SuppliedGood, TierUnlocked of one level with current and required values), `foodsInWindow`, `nearestServiceCost`, `unmetNames`. `multiSourceCosts.ts` - one multi-source Dijkstra over the move costs.
- `supplyOutcome.ts` - the pure accumulator step of FR-009. `suppliedGoods.ts` - `demandedGroups` (union of the current and the next level), `groupStock`, `runSupplyStep` (consumes the household's goods, `housing.goods.consumed`). `advanceStreaks.ts` - the pure streak rules (FR-011).
- The evaluation, in order: `clearInvalidHomes.ts`, `suppliedGoods.ts`, `evaluateDwelling.ts` (requirements, streaks, one level change), `enforceCapacity.ts` (`evictResident`, `evictionOrder`), `collectRent.ts` (`splitRent`, `collectRent`), `houseHomeless.ts` (`freeDwellings`, `houseHomeless`), `admitSettlers.ts` (`spawnArrival`, `admitSettlers`) with `arrivalCell.ts`; `runHousingEvaluation.ts` runs them for one tick.
- `fetchHouseholdGoods.ts` - the task `housing.fetch`, the behavior condition `household_needs_goods` and action `fetch_household_goods` (in `basic_needs` before `claim_job`). `householdDemand.ts` - `householdShortfalls`, `planFetch`, `fetchInProgress`.
- `subscribeHousing.ts` - the food record (`need.item.consumed`), zone split / merge / delete, the before-delete hook that remembers a removed dwelling's level. `HousingService.ts` / `housingServiceRegistry.ts` - the unsaved per-engine cache of the last evaluation (for the status provider) and why settlers could not come.
- `dwellingProvider.ts` - the status provider of subject kind `Dwelling`. `housingViews.ts` - `countDwellingsAtOrAbove`, `buildDwellingSummaries`, `buildDwellingView`, `buildHousingTotals`. `registerHousing.ts` - component, slot-13 system, queries `dwellings`, `dwelling {id}`, `housing`, `dwellings-at-or-above {level}`, the settlement's dwelling counter, the bed policy, the fetch handlers. There are no commands: designate a zone.
- `testHousingWorld.ts` - test helper: a trade world with walled `dwelling(...)` rooms, `throneRoom(...)`, `runEvaluations(n)` and `contentWithLevels(patches)`.

## Rules

- **Evaluation order (FR-006)**: clear invalid homes, consume supplied goods, requirements and streaks, capacity, rent, house the homeless (ascending id into the highest level, then the lowest zone id), settlers. Dwellings are processed by ascending zone id. An inactive or uninhabited dwelling consumes nothing, keeps its streaks and pays nothing.
- **Levels (FR-011)**: `upgradeGraceDays` (3) qualifying days raise the level by one, `downgradeGraceDays` (7) failing days of the current level lower it by one (never below Hovel); both streaks reset on a change; the first failing day queues `housing.dwelling.at-risk`. A level whose `unlockTier` the settlement has not reached cannot be entered (`TierUnlocked`, status `LockedByTier`).
- **Supplied goods (FR-009, FR-010)**: a household demands the goods of its level and of the next, `residents x perResidentPerDay` milli-units a day into an accumulator per group; whole units are taken from the dwelling's storage furniture (materials in group order, furniture by ascending id) when all of them are there, otherwise nothing is taken and the debt is capped at one day (D-58). One resident at a time fetches what is short from storage outside the household (never a board posting).
- **Household reservation (FR-017)**: storage furniture on dwelling tiles is not settlement stock and is offered only to residents (`listStorageFor`); beds there belong to the residents, who prefer them.
- **Rent (FR-013)**: one coin per resident per round from the residents into the treasury through `receiveRent`; a shortfall is reported and not carried over. **Settlers (FR-015)**: `min(free slots, 2)` a day after the homeless are housed, from the level's weighted prototype table, on the arrival cell nearest the throne room; `Arrived` is a Minor moment.
- **Save and load**: the `Dwelling` component, the citizens' homes and the PRNG stream are saved; everything derived (requirement status, the status cache) is rebuilt, and a loaded game upgrades on the same day as one that was never saved.
