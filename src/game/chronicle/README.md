# src/game/chronicle

Moments, journals and the settlement chronicle (spec 028 remainder, DECISIONS D-17 and D-60, plan task 4.6). Every notable thing that happens to a settlement citizen or to the settlement is recorded once as a `MomentRecord`: into the citizen's journal (`Identity.journal`, bounded) and, when the kind is Major, into the settlement chronicle (`SettlementChronicle.moments`, bounded). The engine registers it for itself after the standing system (`registerChronicle` in the `GameEngine` constructor) and registers the death hook right after the identity system (`registerDeathHook`).

- `chronicleTypes.ts` - the system id, the event `chronicle.moment.recorded`, `MomentProminence` (`Minor`, `Major`), `prominenceOfKind` (D-17: Major are `MasteryAchieved`, `BecameFinest`, `TookOffice`, `Died`, `SettlementMilestone`, `TierReached`; `Arrived` is Minor), `MomentRecord`, the typed params of every kind (`TitleEarnedParams`, `FinestParams`, `GuildParams`, `OfficeParams`, `FirstWorkParams`, `FirstTradeParams`, `HomeImprovedParams`, `RenamedParams`, `DiedParams`, `SettlementMilestoneParams`, `TierReachedParams`) and `MomentInput`, the union of kind + citizen + params that sources hand to `recordMoment`. `momentRecordSchema.ts` - the strict Zod schema shared by the journal and the chronicle component (the prominence must be the kind's).
- `recordMoment.ts` - `recordMoment` (id from the root counter `nextMomentId`, tick, styled name at that moment; journal for every kind but `Died`, chronicle for Major, the event) and `appendToJournal` (oldest non-`Arrived` entry dropped beyond `journalCapacity`). `chronicleOf.ts` - the live `SettlementChronicle` data.
- `subscribeMoments.ts` - the moment sources: membership (Arrived with the name re-check, JoinedGuild, LeftGuild), title changes (TitleEarned, MasteryAchieved), skill growth (finest table, `FirstWork` once per skill via `Identity.seenSkills`), `trade.completed` (`FirstTrade` once per citizen via `Identity.tradeSeen`), leader changes and Steward appointments (TookOffice, LostOffice), `housing.dwelling.upgraded` (HomeImproved for each resident), milestones and tiers. `recheckName.ts` - the D-17 ordinal re-check when a named citizen joins the government.
- `trackFinest.ts` - `trackFinest` (FR-015: strictly exceeding the holder at `finestMinimumLevel`, announcement only after `finestCooldownDays`, else a silent change; an incumbent who started skilled is entered silently) and `refreshFinest` (a holder who died or left is replaced by a scan, no moments).
- `registerDeathHook.ts` - the before-delete hook that records `Died` with the styled name `entity.deleted` carries and `journalExcerpt` (the kinds of the last four journal entries); only members of the player government get one.
- `renameCitizen.ts` - `renameCitizen` (FR-020: 1 to 40 characters, byname 0 to 40, no control characters, the ordinal rule without a redraw, `identity.named`, `Renamed {previousName}`; errors `InvalidName` and `UnknownEntity` as `IdentityError`).
- `formatMoment.ts` - the pure text helper: the content template of the kind with `{name}` (the snapshot), `{noun}`, `{nounLower}`, `{skillName}`, `{guildName}`, `{office}`, `{milestone}`, `{tier}`, `{dwellingLevel}`, `{previousName}`, `{day}` and `{settlementNoun}`. Text is never stored.
- `chronicleViews.ts` - `buildChronicleView` (newest first, filter by citizen, also a dead one, and kind), `buildJournalView`, `buildMomentsSince`, `toMomentView`, `tierAtTick` (the tier in force at a record's tick, so a hamlet-day moment keeps saying "hamlet" after the promotion). `registerChronicle.ts` - the subscriptions, the command `RenameCitizen {entityId, givenName, byname}` and the queries `chronicle {entityId?, kind?, limit?}`, `journal {entityId}` and `moments-since {tick}`. `testChronicleWorld.ts` - test helper: a bare game with named citizens of the government.

## Rules

- **One record per event**: each source records once per triggering event; the record has a monotonic id (root counter, mirrored into `SettlementChronicle.nextMomentId`), the tick, the kind, the fixed prominence, the citizen, the styled name at that time and the params (strings and integers only).
- **Members only**: a moment about a citizen is recorded only for a named member of the player government; visitors and NPC leaders get none. `SettlementMilestone` and `TierReached` have no citizen.
- **Journal**: oldest first, at most `journalCapacity` (16); when full the oldest entry other than `Arrived` goes. `FirstWork` and `FirstTrade` do not depend on the journal: the seen skills and the trade flag live in `Identity`, so trimming never repeats them.
- **Chronicle**: Major moments in recording order, at most `chronicleCapacity` (200), the oldest dropped first. Since only Major moments enter it there is nothing to pin. The `chronicle` query shows it newest first.
- **Size (SC-006, D-17)**: 200 full journals plus a full chronicle serialize to about 650 KB in the worst case the test builds; the budget is 1 MB.
- **Died**: recorded synchronously in the delete hook, before the entity is removed, while the offices and the membership are still there. The journal dies with the citizen; the moment keeps a short excerpt and the name.
- **Names**: the Steward's office shows in the styled name (`Godiva the Farmer, Steward of the Settlement`, `stewardTitle` of the name formats); a name snapshot therefore carries the offices of the time.
