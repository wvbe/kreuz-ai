# src/game/production

Production and crafting (spec 014, DECISIONS D-09, D-10 and D-49, plan task 3.3). A workstation is a furniture entity with the `ProductionOrders` component; the player (and later the standing orders of 026) put production orders on it; every order that can start a craft gets a `craft.produce` posting on a job board; a settler claims it, fetches the inputs, locks them, works for `workDuration` ticks, and the outputs land in the workstation inventory from where the haul poster takes them to storage. The engine registers it for itself (`registerProduction` in the `GameEngine` constructor, after zones).

- `productionTypes.ts` - ids and constants (`craftJobId`, `productionPosterIntervalTicks` 6 ...), `OrderStatus`, `ProductionOrder`, `ActiveCraft`, `WorkstationData`, the `production.*` event names and payloads, and the blocked-reason types (`ProductionBlockedKind`, `ProductionBlockedReason`, `CauseRef`, `OrderExplanation`).
- `productionOrdersComponent.ts` - the `ProductionOrders` component `{orders[], craft|null}` with strict Zod schemas (entities save section; the prototypes `oven`, `grinding_mill`, `sawmill`, `workbench` carry it).
- `productionQueries.ts` - `listWorkstations`, `findWorkstation` / `requireWorkstation`, `canMake`, `compatibleRecipes`, `capableWorkstations(engine, recipeId)` (for 026), `findOrder` / `requireOrder`, `isRecipeLocked`.
- `productionOrders.ts` - the order model and the command logic: `createProductionOrder(engine, {workstationId?, recipeId, quantity, priority?})` (the call standing orders reuse), `cancelProductionOrder`, `cancelOrderAt`, `setProductionOrderPaused`, `setProductionOrderPriority`, `cancelCraft`, `pruneFinishedOrders`.
- `productionBlockers.ts` - blocked-reason reporting for spec 025: `orderBlockers`, `explainOrder(engine, orderId)`, `explainWorkstation(engine, workstationId)`, `availableForCraft`, `hasQualifiedWorker`, `inputProducer`.
- `craftExecutor.ts` - the `craft.produce` executor (`createCraftExecutor`, `registerCrafting`). `craftCleanup.ts` - `interruptCraft`, `cancelCraftTask`. `firstOutputMisfit.ts` - `firstOutputMisfit` (do the outputs fit?).
- `productionPoster.ts` - the slot-8 poster: `postCraftJobs`, `postOutputHauls`, `sweepStaleCrafts`. `destroyWorkstation.ts` - what a deleted workstation does (cancel orders, interrupt the craft, drop the contents as a `loose_pile`).
- `productionViews.ts` - the views behind the queries. `registerProduction.ts` - `registerProduction(engine)`: component, executor, delete hook, system `production` (slot 8), the commands `CreateProductionOrder`, `CancelProductionOrder`, `SetProductionOrderPaused`, `SetProductionOrderPriority`, `CancelCraft` and the queries `production-orders {workstationId?}`, `order {orderId}`, `recipes-for {workstationId}`, `workstations {}`.
- `ProductionError.ts` - `ProductionError` / `ProductionErrorKind`. `testProductionWorld.ts` - test helper: a zone test world with `station`, `settler`, `feed`, `order`, `data`, `seen`, plus `contentWithRecipes`.

## Rules

- **Orders (D-10).** `ProductionOrder {orderId, workstationId, recipeId, quantity, remaining, priority 0..100, status Active|Paused|Completed|Cancelled, postingId, createdTick}` lives in the workstation's component, ascending by id (at most 16 finished ones are remembered). Ids come from the persisted `nextOrderId`. `quantity` is the number of crafts; each craft counts `remaining` down. Standing orders ("keep N in stock", 026) are not part of this module: they call `createProductionOrder` for their runs and read `remaining` / `status`, and pick a station with `capableWorkstations`.
- **Variants.** Not supported (D-10): a recipe variant is a separate recipe entry. Recipes are content; compatibility is the recipe's `workstationTag` against the furniture tags. `roomZoneId` is the room restriction (the workstation must stand on a tile of an **active** zone of that type, `isInActiveZoneOfType`), `minSkillLevel` (new, default 0) the least level of the recipe skill, `toolMaterialIds` tools that are locked (`Tool`) during the craft and kept.
- **Posting.** Every 6 ticks, for every workstation that is not crafting and has no posting out, the highest-priority active order that nothing blocks (`orderBlockers` empty) gets one single-craft `craft.produce` posting on the nearest running board (D-08). One posting means one crafter per workstation. The posting target is the workstation cell and entity; the order points back with `postingId`. Priority follows the order's, `MinSkill` is added for skilled recipes.
- **Crafting (014 FR-005, FR-013).** The crafter takes what the work inventory lacks from claimable storage (`findSources`, nearest first, all or nothing per source, no reservation while walking), deposits it, then **locks** inputs (`Lock`) and tools (`Tool`) on the workstation inventory, fixes `workDuration(content, crafter, recipe, durationTicks)` and waits (serialized tick wait). On completion the locks are consumed (tools released), the outputs go into the workstation inventory plus `rollOutputBonus` extra units on the first output (units that do not fit are not made), `skill.work.completed` is emitted for the recipe skill. Recipes have a positive duration, so there is no 0-tick craft. The work inventory is the workstation's (`queryable: false`, so waiting inputs are no stock); hand-crafting without a workstation is not modelled (every recipe names a workstation tag). Outputs always go to the workstation (no `OutputDestination` yet); `postOutputHauls` posts `haul.deliver` for everything in a work inventory that no unfinished order needs, routed per 018.
- **Cancel (D-10).** `CancelProductionOrder` stops the order: status `Cancelled`, an open posting is withdrawn, a crafter still fetching stops (it hands carried goods back), but a craft that **already runs finishes** (outputs are made and counted, the order stays `Cancelled`). `CancelCraft {workstationId}` **interrupts**: locks released, nothing consumed, progress back to 0, `production.crafting.interrupted`, the order and its posting stay (the next crafter starts again). A critical need, the crafter's deletion or the posting vanishing interrupt the same way (`sweepStaleCrafts` catches what the task hook cannot). A deleted workstation cancels its orders, interrupts and drops its contents as a loose pile.
- **Conservation.** Goods are always in a source, in the crafter's inventory (and task record), in the work inventory or under a lock; consumption (`commit(lock, null)`) and output placement happen in one step. The tests count every material over all inventories against the completed events.
- **Blocked reasons (025).** `explainOrder` returns `{orderId, workstationId, status, reasons[]}`; reasons are `{kind, params, causeRef}` in the 025 order: `LockedByTier`, `MissingWorkstation{workstationTag}`, `MissingRoom{zoneTypeId}` (causeRef the zone), `NoQualifiedWorker{skillId, requiredLevel}`, `MissingTool{tag}`, `MissingInput{materialId, required, available, noProducer}` (causeRef the first stalled producer's workstation), `OutputBlocked{materialId}`; a paused order reports `Paused{productionOrderId}`. `explainWorkstation` adds `NoOrders` (Idle). Pure, derived on demand.
- **Events (section 4.4).** `production.order.created|completed|cancelled {orderId, workstationId, recipeId}`, `production.crafting.started {workstationId, crafterId, recipeId}`, `.completed {..., inputs, outputs}` (actual quantities), `.interrupted` / `.failed {..., reason}`, `production.output.blocked {workstationId, crafterId, materialId}` (once per craft).
