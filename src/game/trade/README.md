# src/game/trade

Trade and currency (spec 019, DECISIONS D-12, D-13 and D-55, plan task 4.1): the settlement treasury, wages paid from it, travelling traders, negotiated atomic trades, the trader refined-credit ledger and the player's trade orders. The engine registers it for itself (`registerTrade` in the `GameEngine` constructor, after gathering).

- `tradeTypes.ts` - system ids, the stream name `trade.visit`, job ids, constants, the enums `OrderDirection`, `OrderStatus`, `OfferStatus`, `RejectReason`, `ExecutionFailure`, `TraderPhase`, the types `Item`, `Leg`, `TradeOffer`, `TradeOrder`, `LedgerEntry`, `VisitRecord`, `PendingWage`, `RefineRule` and every event name and payload (`trade.*`, `trader.*`, `treasury.*`).
- `merchantComponent.ts` / `traderComponent.ts` - the components `Merchant` (`sellsItems`, `priceMultiplierMilli`, `minimumMarginRatePermille`, on any entity) and `Trader` (content of a caravan: purse, bid and ceiling permille, goods it sells, materials it buys, `refines` rules; visit state). The caravan prototype `trader_caravan` lives in `content/data/engine-prototypes.json`.
- `TradeService.ts` / `tradeServiceRegistry.ts` - offers, orders, the refined-credit ledger, the visit schedule and the daily standing gains, saved in the section `systems.trade`. `TreasuryService.ts` / `treasuryServiceRegistry.ts` - queued wages, section `systems.treasury`.
- `treasury.ts` - the treasury (the inventory of the government faction entity): `treasuryEntity`, `installTreasury`, `treasuryBalance`, `creditTreasury`, `payFromTreasury`, `depositToTreasury`, `receiveRent` (the hook of task 4.5), `payWageFromTreasury` (the wage payer of the job boards), `retryWagePayments`.
- `tradePricing.ts` - the price functions: `valueOfItemsMilli`, `scarcityPermille`, `effectiveMultiplierPermille`, `minAcceptableMilli`, `wholeCoins`, `traderBidCoins`, `traderCeilingCoins`.
- `tradeEvaluation.ts` - the seller's decision: `evaluateOffer` (reject / accept / counter), `askedMilli`, `sellerMarginPermille` (content margin plus the Greedy trait through `marginAddPermille`), `isFactionHostile`, `isTradeHostile`, `hasTradeAgreement`.
- `tradeExecution.ts` - `executeTrade`: all legs reserved (`Payment` reservations), committed, rolled back when any fails; `legsOf`.
- `tradeOffers.ts` - the negotiation: `proposeOffer`, `resolveOffer`, `processOffers` (slot 10, ascending offer id), `acceptCounter`, `cancelOffer`, `cancelOffersOf`, `cancelHostileOffers`, `negotiate` (all rounds in one go, for settlers standing at the trader).
- `tradeSettlement.ts` - what follows a trade: `settleTrade` (ledger, flow, standing, `trade.completed`), `recordTradeFlow` (`FlowSource.Trade`), `isSettlementSide`. `applyTradeStanding.ts` - the standing hook (+1 per trade, 5 a day).
- `refinedLedger.ts` - the owner's rule (D-13): `grantRefinedCredit`, `drawRefinedCredit`, `refinedCreditMilli`, `refinedAllowance`, `isRefinedGood`, `topUpRefinedStash`.
- `traderVisits.ts` - the visit cycle: `initTraderVisits`, `runTraderVisits` (slot 11), `spawnTrader`, `pickEntryCell`, `restockTrader`, `marketCell`, `listTraders`, `presentTrader`, `traderPrototypeIds`. `createTraderVisitTask.ts` - the `trader.visit` task (walk in, stay, walk out, leave).
- `tradeQuotes.ts` - `quoteTrade`, `sellableQuantity`. `tradeOrders.ts` - `createTradeOrder`, `cancelTradeOrder`, `recordTrip`, `recordTripFailure`, `isStockRace`, `orderWait`, `postTradeJobs`, `jobTypeOfDirection`.
- `tradeSellJob.ts` / `tradeBuyJob.ts` - the executors of `trade.sell` and `trade.buy`; `tradeJobSupport.ts` - their shared helpers.
- `tradeViews.ts` - the queries `traders`, `trade-offers`, `trade-orders`, `trade-ledger`, `treasury`, `trade-quote`. `registerTrade.ts` - components, task, executors, wage payer, sections, systems (`treasury` and `trade` in slot 10, `trade.visits` in slot 11), hooks, commands and queries. `TradeError.ts` - command failures. `testTradeWorld.ts` - test helper: a storage test world with `trader`, `settler`, `coins`, `treasury`, `command`, `query`, `record`.

## Rules

- **Money.** Coins are whole `silver_penny` items (D-04). The treasury is the inventory of the government faction entity (there is no Throne Room yet); a new game starts with `startingTreasury` (1000) coins. The wage payer takes wages from it; a wage the treasury or the worker's inventory cannot take is queued by claim id (`treasury.payment.deferred`) and retried every tick in id order (`.completed`).
- **Prices** (D-12, D-55). A seller asks `ceil(value x multiplier x (1000 + margin) / 1e6)` milli-coins, rounded up to whole coins; multiplier = the seller's `priceMultiplier` x the agreement discount (0.9 while a trade agreement stands) x a scarcity premium (up to +20 % for a trader that sold its stock down); margin = `minimumMarginRate` (0.1) + the trait margin of the seller (Greedy +0.05). Barter goods count at their plain value. A trader buys at a bid of 1.15 x value and pays up to 1.25 x after a counter. A counter asks the missing coins; the buyer may accept up to `maxNegotiationRounds` (3); offers expire after `offerTimeoutTicks` (24).
- **Atomic.** Reserve every leg, commit them one after the other, move the done ones back on any failure (`trade.execution.failed`).
- **Traders.** Caravans are not citizens (no population, no AI); they carry `Trader` and a faction reference. A kind arrives on day `traderVisitStartDay` (3) plus a jitter from the stream `trade.visit`, enters at a far reachable cell, walks to the first job board, stays `traderStayDays` (2), walks out and is deleted; the next one comes `traderVisitIntervalDays` (6) later. Refilled at every arrival: purse, goods it sells.
- **Refined credit** (owner rule, D-13). Selling raw goods to a trader that has a `refines` rule adds `quantity x ratioMilli` milli-items of credit for the refined good at once; the settlement can buy at most `floor(credit / 1000)` of it and each purchase draws it down. Credit is saved per settlement and trader kind and never expires; the refined stock a trader brings back is `floor(credit / 1000)`.
- **Orders.** The player trades only through orders (`TradeSell` / `TradeBuy`, no trade-policy screen): `postTradeJobs` posts one trip at a time on the nearest board while the trader is at the market, a settler carries the goods (sell) or the coins from the treasury (buy), negotiates with the trader at its cell, hands the proceeds in and books the trip. Purchases are hauled into storage by `haul.deliver`.
