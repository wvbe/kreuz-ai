# src/game/status/flow

The production flow ledger (spec 025 FR-011 to FR-013, FR-018, DECISIONS D-51): how many units of each material are produced and consumed per game day, by whom and from which source, plus the Flow view derived from it.

- `FlowLedger.ts` - the ledger: `record(day, entry)`, `prune(currentDay)`, `dayList()`; root save section `productionLedger` `{days: [{day, entries: [{materialId, direction, source, subject, quantity}]}]}` (ints and strings only; entries in canonical order, so saves are byte-identical).
- `registerFlowEvents.ts` - `registerFlowEvents(engine)`: the subscriptions that feed the ledger from events that already exist. `production.crafting.completed` (inputs consumed, outputs produced, `Recipe`, subject the workstation), `construction.job.completed` (`consumed` -> `Construction`, `yield` -> `Deconstruction`, subject the site), `jobboard.job.completed` (`outputs` of job types that list `outputs` in the content: `Gathering`, subject the worker; hauling reports the goods it moved as outputs too and is deliberately not counted), `need.item.consumed` (`NeedConsumption`), `inventory.item.expired` (`Spoilage`, subject the holder when it is a status subject), `housing.goods.consumed` (`HouseholdConsumption`, subject `Dwelling`; the event comes with task 4.5).
- `recordFlow.ts` - `recordFlow(engine, entry)`: count units on the day of the tick being processed. The hook for sources without an event of their own: the trade task (4.1) calls it with `FlowSource.Trade` for sales and purchases of the player settlement (D-18: exactly one party is the player's). Never record hauling, transfers, wages or treasury payments.
- `flowSummary.ts` - `buildFlowRows(engine)` (query `flow`) and `buildFlowRow(engine, materialId)` (query `flow-of`).

## Rules

- **Day bucket.** The ledger is fed by the slot-20 drain, so the bucket is `toDay(tick)` of the tick being processed; at tick-of-day 0 a new bucket starts. The slot-19 system `status.ledger` drops the days older than `ledgerWindowDays` (7) complete days before the current one, so the ledger holds at most 8 days.
- **Flow row.** Per material with counts in the window: `producedPerDayMilli`, `consumedPerDayMilli`, `netPerDayMilli` (x1000 averages over the complete days of the window; while no day is complete yet the current day alone, a deviation from FR-013 so that the first day is not empty), `stock` (all claimable storage, `stockOf`), `daysOfSupplyMilli` (`stock / -net` x1000, null unless the net is negative), `trend` (daily net, oldest first, the current day last), `windowProduced` / `windowConsumed` (totals over the kept days), and the `producers` / `consumers` as `{subject, source, quantity}` largest first. The list is sorted largest deficit first (net ascending, then material id).
- **Conservation.** For a window that covers the whole period, `produced - consumed` equals the change of the material's total over all inventories (tested over 600 ticks of a bakery colony for wheat, flour and bread).
