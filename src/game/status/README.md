# src/game/status

Status explanations and production flow (spec 025, DECISIONS D-18 and D-51, plan task 3.6). Every stall-able subject (citizen, workstation, production order, build site, zone, job board, job posting, loose pile) has a **derived** status: Active, Idle or Blocked, an ordered list of structured reasons (`{kind, params, causeRef}`, never text) and, for a busy subject, an activity. Nothing is reported or stored per reason: the owning systems expose pure derivation functions, a provider per subject kind calls them, and `explain` follows the `causeRef` links to answer "why?". The only stored state is the settle tracker (when a stall began, what has been published) and the flow ledger. The engine registers all of it for itself (`registerStatus` in the `GameEngine` constructor, after construction).

- `statusTypes.ts` - ids and constants (`statusGraceTicks` 12, `ledgerWindowDays` 7, `maxExplanationDepth` 5, the `status.*` event names), the enums `StatusState`, `StatusSubjectKind`, `BlockedReasonKind` (the 23 kinds of spec 025 in precedence order plus `AwaitingDecision` and `Unexplained`), `ActivityKind`, `ChainEnd`, `FlowDirection`, `FlowSource`, and the data types `StatusSubjectRef {kind, id}`, `Reason`, `SubjectStatus`, `StatusProvider`, `Explanation`, `StatusRecord`, `IdleBlockedRow`, `FlowRow` and the event payloads.
- `reasons.ts` - `makeReason`, `reasonKey` (kind plus the first identifying param, so counters such as `available` never count as a change), `statusKey`, `isImmediateReason`, `sortReasons` (FR-004 precedence), `toReasonKind`, `refKey`.
- `StatusService.ts` / `statusServiceRegistry.ts` - the per-engine registry: `registerProvider(provider)`, the tracker and the ledger; `getStatusService(engine)` finds it.
- `explain.ts` - `evaluateSubject(engine, ref, context?)` (provider call, sorted reasons, `Unexplained` fallback) and `explain(engine, ref)` (primary reason plus the chain of causes, with cycle protection and the depth cap).
- `statusContext.ts` - `createStatusContext(engine)`: per-pass helpers for providers; the reach costs of a citizen come from a derived cache keyed by start cell and `GameMap.revision` (never saved, never changes a result).
- `StatusTracker.ts` - the settle tracker, root save section `statuses`: `observe(ref, status, tick)`, `retainOnly`, `purgeOrphans`, `get`, `records`.
- `statusEvaluation.ts` - `listSubjects` and `runStatusPass` (the slot-18 system): evaluate every subject, feed the tracker, emit `status.blocked` / `status.unblocked`.
- `idleBlocked.ts` - `buildIdleBlockedView(engine, options)`: the Idle & Blocked list. `subjectOfEntity.ts` - the subject an entity is (for `why <entityId>`).
- `registerStatus.ts` - `registerStatus(engine)`: providers, save sections, the slot-18 system `status` and the slot-19 system `status.ledger`, the ledger subscriptions, the queries `explain {id, kind?}`, `idle-blocked {includeUnsettled?, state?, kind?}`, `flow {}` and `flow-of {materialId}`. There are no commands.
- `testStatusWorld.ts` - test helper: a construction world with a synthetic provider for the kind `StandingOrder` (no built-in provider before 4.3) and `bakery()`.
- [providers](providers/README.md) - the built-in providers. [flow](flow/README.md) - the flow ledger.

## Rules

- **Pull, not push (D-18).** A provider is `{kind, subjects(engine), evaluate(engine, ref, context)}`; it is pure (reads the game, never stores). Register your own with `getStatusService(engine).registerProvider(provider)` from your `register*` function (kinds are unique; `StandingOrder` and `Dwelling` are free for tasks 4.3 and 4.5). Reasons of a provider are listed by the provider; `evaluateSubject` sorts them by the FR-004 precedence (stable inside a kind, so recipe-input order survives). A non-Active subject without a reason is shown as `Unexplained`, never silent; the soak tests assert it never happens.
- **States.** Active (with `activity` for a citizen that works, sleeps, eats or walks to a board, a workstation that crafts, a claimed posting); Idle only for a citizen with no work and a workstation with `NoOrders`; Blocked for everything else that is not Active.
- **Settling (FR-006/007, D-18).** A non-Active state or a new primary reason is published only after it held for `statusGraceTicks` (12) ticks in a row, except `Paused`, `NoSeatOfGovernment`, `NoSteward`, `LockedByTier`, `ScopeZoneMissing`, `NoOrders` (at once). Becoming Active is published at once. A pending change that is replaced restarts its timer while the old published reason stays published. Only a change of the published primary reason (kind plus identifying param) re-emits `status.blocked` (with `previousReason` and the unchanged `sinceTick`); `status.unblocked {stalledTicks, removed}` when a settled stall ends or the subject disappears. Events go through the bus in slot 18, so subscribers see them in the same tick's drain.
- **`sinceTick`.** The first tick of the whole continuous stall (it does not move while the primary reason changes inside the stall); the record also keeps `reasonSinceTick`, the first tick of the current primary reason. A stall that ends before the grace period leaves no event and no trace.
- **Explain (FR-010).** `chain[0]` is the subject, each next link is the subject that the previous link's primary reason points at with `causeRef`, with its own primary reason. It stops at a repeated subject (`Cycle`), after `maxExplanationDepth` causes (`DepthCap`), at a cause that no longer exists (`Gone`) or when there is no cause (`Complete`). Examples: an oven's `MissingInput flour` points at the mill; a mill's order that is posted points at the posting; a posting nobody takes explains itself (`NoQualifiedWorker`, `Unreachable`, `Paused` -> board -> `ZoneInactive` -> zone -> `ZoneRequirementsUnmet`).
- **Load (FR-014).** The tracker is saved in `statuses` and restored as is; the next pass re-derives the status and, for the same primary reason, keeps `sinceTick` and the settle progress and emits nothing. Records of subjects that no longer exist are dropped silently after a load.
- **Cost.** The pass runs every tick over every subject; idle citizens share one reach search per start cell and map revision. About twice the cost of the same game without it (the plain 2-day seed-42 run is dominated by the AI), well inside the 2 ms per tick of SC-003 for a six-citizen colony.
