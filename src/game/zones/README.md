# src/game/zones

Zones and rooms (spec 015, DECISIONS D-11 and D-48, plan task 3.4). A zone is an entity with the `Zone` component: a typed set of cells on one map (square or Voronoi, joined by cell adjacency). The zones system derives, every tick, whether a zone is a room, which requirements it meets and what its status is, and announces changes with `zone.*` events. The engine registers it for itself after storage (`registerZones` in the `GameEngine` constructor), so every game has it.

- `zoneTypes.ts` - ids, event names, `ZoneStatus` (`Active, Incomplete, Inactive`), `ZoneGapKind` (`NotEnclosed, TooSmall, MissingFurniture, MissingJobBoard`), `ZoneGap`, `FurnitureRequirement` / `FurnitureAlternative`, `ZoneData`, `MergeOffer`, `ZoneView`.
- `zoneComponent.ts` - the `Zone` component `{zoneTypeId, mapId, tiles, isRoom, active, status, gaps, filter, createdTick, activeSinceTick}` (the `zone` engine prototype carries it; entity id = zone id). `ZoneError.ts` - `ZoneError` / `ZoneErrorKind`.
- `furnitureRequirements.ts` - the requirement grammar: `parseFurnitureRequirements` (text to typed predicate), `formatRequirement`, `compileRequirements` (content zone type to predicate), `requiredPieces`, `countMatching`, `checkRequirement`.
- `zoneGeometry.ts` - `connectedComponents`, `outerRing` (grid-agnostic, through `map.neighbors`).
- `zoneEvaluation.ts` - `evaluateZone` (room, status, gaps, furniture, boards), `isEnclosed`, `isEnclosingCell`, `enclosingRingCells`, `isBorderCell`.
- `ZoneService.ts` / `zoneServiceRegistry.ts` - the per-engine service: cell index, designate / add / remove / delete / merge, the slot-9 `evaluateAll`, merge offers (section `systems.zones`), board pausing, the storage hooks. `getZoneService(engine)` finds it.
- `zoneQueries.ts` - `zoneAt`, `zoneOfEntity`, `activeZonesOfType`, `isInActiveZoneOfType`, `isActivityPermittedAt` (FR-009), `zoneModifierMilliFor` (FR-010; `ai/mood/runMood` reads `mood.bonus`, D-211). `zoneAffinity.ts` - `zoneWorkers`, `zoneAffinity`. `zoneViews.ts` - the views behind the queries.
- `registerZones.ts` - `registerZones(engine)`: component, section, slot-9 system, commands and queries. `testZoneWorld.ts` - test helper: a storage test world with `designate`, `walls`, `door`, `furniture`, `rect`, `events`, `setTier` and `contentWithZones`.

## Rules

- **Timing (DECISIONS D-11).** Commands run in slot 1 and only change tiles. Slot 9 of the same tick re-derives every zone from the world (tiles, walls and doors, furniture, boards): status, gaps and `zone.requirements.met` / `.lost` / `zone.room.changed` appear in that tick. Every zone is re-derived each tick (a pass over 200 walled zones takes a few milliseconds, spec 015 SC-008 is 20 ms), so there is no stale dirty flag. **Effects** (the decay modifier of a Pantry, `isInActiveZoneOfType`) apply from the **next** tick: they need `tickCount > activeSinceTick`. After `newGame` / `loadGame` the derived state is rebuilt **silently** (no events, FR-013); the saved `active`, `status` and `gaps` are ignored in favour of the world.
- **Status.** `Active` = no gap. `Inactive` = a structural gap (`TooSmall`, `NotEnclosed`). `Incomplete` = size and room are fine but furniture or a job board is missing. `active` (the flag behind the events) is `status === Active`; `zone.requirements.lost {zoneId, zoneTypeId, gaps}` fires on active to not active.
- **Room.** Every cell around the zone (outside it) holds a `wall` or `door` entity (open or closed) or a wall obstruction; the map border is never enclosed; cells of other zones do not enclose.
- **Furniture.** Counts when its entity (`Furniture` component, `furnitureId`) stands on a zone tile; build sites are no furniture. Grammar: `any bed`, `2x tag:chair`, `id:table`, `2x id:crate or 2x id:chest` (OR), `any oven and any bed` (AND), `1x tag:bed per 8 tiles` (density: per started 8 tiles). Authored content uses the equivalent `furnitureRequirements` (inner list = OR, outer = AND, optional `perTiles`); `zone types` can also set `requiresJobBoard`.
- **Designation.** `DesignateZone` splits painted cells into one zone per connected component (ids ascending by lowest cell); a cell already in another zone rejects the command unless `reassign` moves it; locked tiers give `ContentLocked`. `RemoveZoneTiles` that disconnects a zone splits it (largest part keeps the id, ties lowest cell). `AddZoneTiles` cells that do not touch the zone become zones of their own.
- **Merge.** When a wall that enclosed a zone disappears and the freed cell touches another zone of the same type, `MergeOffer {offerId, zoneAId, zoneBId}` is made (`zone.merge.offered`); `ConfirmZoneMerge {offerId, accept}` merges into the lower id, taking the free cells between so the zone stays contiguous. An offer lapses when a zone is deleted or changes size.
- **Affinity and storage.** `zoneAffinity` is the best familiarity bucket (D-43) of the citizens standing in the zone for the zone type's `skillAffinityId`. Storage routing tiers 0 (`zoneId` of the request) and 1 (a hauler with level >= 20 in the zone's skill; works while the zone is inactive) use the zone of the storage's cell; a zone filter applies to storage without a filter of its own; stockpile zones make their furniture storage stockpiles; furniture on `dwelling` zones is never offered.
- **Boards.** A `job_board` entity on a tile of a zone that is not active is paused with `PauseSource.System`; the pause is lifted when the zone is active again or deleted. The zones remember (in `systems.zones`) which boards they paused, so other pauses are never lifted.
