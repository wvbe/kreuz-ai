# src/game/storage

Stockpiles, reservations and hauling (spec 018, DECISIONS D-09, D-26 and D-47, plan task 3.2). Storage furniture are ordinary entities with an `Inventory`; this folder adds the reservation primitive that crafting, construction and trade will use, the routing of goods to storage, the hauling job `haul.deliver` that moves loose goods there, and the stock queries. The engine registers it for itself (`registerStorage` in the `GameEngine` constructor), so every game has it.

- `storageTypes.ts` - ids and constants (`haulJobId`, `haulPosterIntervalTicks` 24 ...), `MaterialFilter`, `StockpileData`, `FurnitureData`, `ReservationKind` (`Lock, Haul, Tool, Payment`), `Reservation`, `StockSummary`, `StockSource`, the event `storage.no-compatible-destination`.
- `furnitureComponent.ts` - the minimal `Furniture` component `{furnitureId}` (furniture entity = `Position` + `Furniture` + optional `Inventory`; zones 3.4 and construction 3.5 extend it). `stockpileComponent.ts` - the `Stockpile` component `{priority 0..100, filter|null}` (the `chest` prototype carries both).
- `materialFilter.ts` - `normalizeFilter`, `assertFilterKnown`, `filterAccepts`, `effectiveFilter` (own filter replaces the furniture default).
- `ReservationService.ts` - reserve / reserveUpTo / release / releaseHolder / releaseInventory / commit, `availableTo`, `reservedQuantity`, `reconcile`; save section `systems.reservations`.
- `StorageService.ts` / `storageServiceRegistry.ts` - per-engine state: the reservations, the no-destination marks (section `systems.storage`) and the decay hook (`decayModifierMilli`, `addDecayModifierSource`). `getStorageService(engine)` finds it.
- `storageQueries.ts` - `isStorageEntity`, `listStorage`, `canRetrieveFrom`, `canDepositInto`, `stockOf`, `findSources`. `storageRouting.ts` - `routeCandidates`, `chooseRoute`, `compareRoutes`, `RouteTier`.
- `haulDeliver.ts` - the `haul.deliver` executor (`createHaulExecutor`, `registerHauling`). `haulPoster.ts` - `postHaulJob`, `postHaulJobs` (the auto-poster), `nearestRunningBoard` (where system postings go, also used by production), `findLooseGoods`, `haulableMaterialIds`, `releaseOrphanedHaulReservations`.
- `storageNeedSource.ts` - the need source finder (hungry settlers eat bread from stockpiles). `storageViews.ts` - the views behind the queries. `registerStorage.ts` - `registerStorage(engine)`: components, sections, system `storage` (slot 10), commands and queries.
- `StorageError.ts` - `StorageError` / `StorageErrorKind`. `testStorageWorld.ts` - test helper: a job test world with `chest`, `pile`, `give`, `count`.

## Rules

- **Reservations (D-09).** `Reservation {id, kind, holderId, inventoryOwnerId, materialId, quantity, createdTick}`. A new reservation can only take unreserved stock; reserved stock is invisible to everybody except its holder (`availableTo`, `findSources`, the AI item availability). `commit(id, destination)` moves the reserved goods to the destination (freshness kept) or consumes them (`null`) and ends the reservation, all or nothing. A reservation is dropped when its holder or inventory owner is deleted, clamped to the stock every tick (`reconcile`, slot 10) and a `Haul` reservation whose holder has no `haul.deliver` task is released. Ids come from the persisted `nextReservationId`.
- **Queries (018 FR-011, D-09).** Claimable storage = entities with `Furniture` + `Inventory` (queryable) and `loose_pile`s. Excluded: construction sites (`build_site`), `queryable: false` inventories, and everything carried by citizens or creatures. `stock` totals add the reserved part separately. "Locked chest" = the inventory's permission rules deny the requester `Retrieve` (no other lock mechanic); a chest that denies `Store` is not offered as a destination.
- **Routing (018 FR-010, D-26).** A destination must be furniture storage on the same map, reachable, accepting the material (filter, permission) with room for at least a unit. Order: tier (0 storage in the zone the request names (`RouteRequest.zoneId`, `deliverToZoneId`), 1 storage in a zone whose skill affinity matches a hauler of level >= 20, 2 own or default filter matches, 3 stockpile without filter, 4 other furniture storage; currency never goes to tier 4), then stockpile priority desc, path cost asc, free capacity desc, entity id asc. The zones of task 3.4 supply the zone of a cell through `StorageService.setZoneRouteProvider`: tiers 0 and 1, the zone filter (used by storage without a filter of its own, so the own filter overrides it), stockpile zones (their furniture storage counts as a stockpile) and the dwelling exclusion (furniture on `dwelling` tiles is never offered, spec 029). Filters never evict contents (FR-006).
- **Hauling.** `haul.deliver` targets a source entity and a material. The hauler reserves what it can carry (`Haul` reservation), walks to the source, commits the reservation into its own inventory, picks the best storage, walks there and deposits as much as fits; a full storage is excluded and the next one tried (3 times). Goods nothing accepts stay with the hauler, the job completes and the poster tries again. Every step keeps the goods in the source, under a reservation or in the hauler's inventory, so a cancel, failure, save or load never duplicates or loses items.
- **Haul poster.** Every 24 ticks (slot 10) it posts `haul.deliver` on the nearest running board for loose goods without an active posting: everything in a `loose_pile`, and units of job outputs (`haulableMaterialIds`: oak logs, wheat ...) that a citizen carries. Personal belongings (bread, tools, coins, nails) are not outputs and are never taken. Goods without a destination emit `storage.no-compatible-destination` once; open postings whose goods or destination disappeared are cancelled; emptied piles are removed.
- **Decay.** The slot-3 system calls `StorageService.decayModifierMilli(entity)` for the `zoneModifierMilli` of `decayInventory`: the product of the furniture content's `inventory.decay.rate` effects and the registered sources (the Pantry zone of 3.4 adds one: the `inventory.decay.rate` effect of an active zone, from the tick after it became active). 0 stops decay, 500 halves it; difficulty multiplies on top (D-07).
